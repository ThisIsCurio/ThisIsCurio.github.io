Model: weak-field general relativity, rate difference = GM/c² × (1/R − 1/(R + h)) with Earth's GM = 3.986 × 10¹⁴ m³/s², R = 6,371 km. "Lifetime" = 79 years. "1 foot" is NIST's 33 cm. Real satellites also move fast, which slows their clocks (that part is special relativity): GPS nets about +38 µs a day, the space station runs slow overall.
Sources: NIST, 2010 (a clock 33 cm higher runs faster) · R. Pogge, Real-World Relativity: GPS
