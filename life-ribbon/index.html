Your felt halfway point

If each year weighs 1 ÷ your age (Janet's model), 80 years re-space like this: early years stretch, late years squeeze. The middle lands at about age 9.

The good news from the video: years packed with firsts tend to feel longer looking back. So a new place, skill or hobby can make this year stand out.

These are models, not measurements. Janet's proportional idea (1877) is a way of thinking about felt time, not a law. In a survey of 499 people aged 14 to 94 (Wittmann & Lehnhoff, 2005), age only changed how fast the last ten years felt; weeks, months and single years felt the same at every age. "Days at age 5" = 365 × 5 ÷ your age. "Felt" spacing uses log(age) from age 1 to 80.
